# GateGuard AI

## Predictive Airport Gate Allocation & Conflict Prevention

GateGuard AI is a machine-learning-based decision-support prototype designed to predict potential airport gate conflicts and recommend a suitable alternative gate.

The system considers factors such as:

- Current gate occupancy
- Expected aircraft turnaround duration
- Upcoming flights
- Gate availability
- Future conflict risk

## Problem

Airport gates can become unavailable or cause conflicts when aircraft are delayed or their turnaround takes longer than expected.

GateGuard AI aims to identify these potential conflicts early and recommend a better gate before the conflict occurs.

## Proposed Solution

GateGuard AI analyzes flight and gate information, predicts potential conflicts, evaluates available gates, and ranks alternative gates based on their suitability.
